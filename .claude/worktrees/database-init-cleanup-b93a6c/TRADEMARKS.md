# Planvexa Trademark Policy

This policy governs trademark and brand use for Planvexa. Copyright and
software-licence rights are separate from trademark rights.

Rights granted under AGPL-3.0-only apply to the software source code and
other material that is actually licensed under that software licence.
Those rights do not by themselves grant permission to use Planvexa
trademarks, logos, icons, product artwork, official screenshots, domain
names, social-media identities, or official release terminology except as
required for truthful attribution or nominative reference.

## Covered marks and brand assets

This policy covers the following identifiers and related brand assets:

- The Planvexa name.
- Planvexa logos.
- Official icons.
- Product artwork.
- Official screenshots.
- Domain names.
- Social-media identities.
- Official release terminology, including claims that a build is an official Planvexa release.

## Permitted without separate written approval

- Truthful statements that software is based on Planvexa.
- Links to the official Planvexa project at https://github.com/Anawaz/Planvexa.
- Reasonable attribution required by the licence or additional terms.
- Discussion, reviews, tutorials, compatibility statements, and news coverage.
- Unmodified official distributions clearly identified as official builds.

## Written permission required

- Using "Planvexa" as the primary name of a modified distribution.
- Using a Planvexa logo as the main logo of a fork.
- Using confusingly similar branding.
- Registering Planvexa-branded domains, companies, products, or services.
- Presenting a modified release as an official Planvexa release.
- Implying endorsement, certification, sponsorship, partnership, or approval.

## Rebranded forks

A rebranded fork may use its own name and branding, but it must:

- Preserve required copyright and licence notices.
- State that it is based on Planvexa.
- Identify that it is modified.
- Identify the maintainer or distributor.
- Avoid implying that it is the official Planvexa project.
- Avoid claiming authorship of the original Planvexa code.

## No implied trademark licence

Trademark rights are separate from rights granted under AGPL-3.0-only.
Nothing in the software licence, notice files, or additional terms grants
general permission to use Planvexa trademarks or brand assets beyond
reasonable attribution and nominative reference.
